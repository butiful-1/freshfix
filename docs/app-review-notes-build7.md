Thank you for the detailed review of build 6. Build 7 addresses both issues.

GUIDELINE 3.1.1 — IN-APP PURCHASE
• Plus ($14.99/month) and Premium ($24.99/month) are now sold with Apple In-App
  Purchase as auto-renewable subscriptions in one subscription group
  ("Old2New Membership"): app.old2new.ios.plus.monthly and
  app.old2new.ios.premium.monthly.
• "Recipe Upgrades" is our name for a recipe transformation — the metered
  monthly unit (5/month free, 50 on Plus, 150 on Premium). It is not a separate
  product; the subscriptions are the only paid items. There are no credits,
  packs, or one-time purchases.
• Where to find the paywall: sign in → Pricing tab in the bottom bar. The same
  paywall opens from the "Upgrade" button on Home, from the "Plus or Premium
  plan required" card on the Saved tab (PDF cookbook), and from the modal that
  appears when the 5 free transformations are used up.
• To purchase: on the Pricing screen tap "Subscribe to Plus" or "Subscribe to
  Premium". The native StoreKit sheet completes the purchase; the app verifies
  the signed transaction on our server and the plan updates immediately.
• Restore Purchases: Pricing screen (button under the plan cards) and
  About tab → Subscription → Restore Purchases. Manage Subscription (Apple's
  sheet) is on the same two screens for Apple-billed plans.
• Existing customers who subscribed on our website (Stripe) sign in with the
  same account and keep the plan they already pay for, per guideline 3.1.3(b);
  the app does not sell, link to, or promote any purchase method other than
  In-App Purchase. Web-purchased plans show "Purchased on old2new.app" as a
  plain label only.
• Subscription terms (title, length, price, auto-renewal, cancellation) plus
  links to our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy are shown on the Pricing screen
  before purchase.

GUIDELINE 1.4.1 — HEALTH / NUTRITION INFORMATION
• Every health-related recommendation now has a "Sources" control next to it
  that opens the supporting references (USDA FoodData Central, Dietary
  Guidelines for Americans, FDA, NIH/NIDDK, CDC, ADA, peer-reviewed research),
  each a working link that opens in the system browser.
• Sample path: sign in → Home → the "What these mean" control next to the goal
  chips shows what each goal (GLP-1 Friendly, Keto, Mediterranean, High
  Protein, Low Sugar, Low Calorie, Diabetic Friendly) means and its sources.
  Then enter "Chicken Alfredo", select "Diabetic Friendly", tap "Transform My
  Recipe". On the result: the "How we estimate" control under the
  before/after calories explains our nutrition methodology (AI-estimated,
  per-serving, rounded, not measured) with sources; the Swaps tab has a
  "Sources" control beside "Why These Swaps"; the Macros tab has "How we
  estimate · Sources".
• Central references library: About tab → "Sources & References" → Open, or
  "View all references" from any Sources sheet. It lists every source by topic,
  the nutrition methodology, and the definition of each goal. Also published at
  https://old2new.app/references.
• Disclaimer: shown on first launch (must be accepted), on every Sources
  sheet, in About → Health Disclaimer, on the References page, on the Pricing
  screen footer, and on the PDF cookbook cover and final page. Text: "Old2New
  provides general food and nutrition information for educational purposes
  only. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or
  medical condition. Nutrition values may be estimates and individual dietary
  needs vary. Consult a physician, registered dietitian, or other qualified
  healthcare professional before making dietary changes related to a medical
  condition or medication."
• All calorie and macro figures are labelled as estimates; AI-generated text is
  constrained to describe ingredient characteristics (lower in added sugar,
  higher in fiber) and is filtered server-side so it never claims to treat,
  prevent, control, or be "safe for" any medical condition or medication.
