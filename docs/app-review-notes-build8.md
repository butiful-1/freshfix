Hello App Review — thank you for the detailed review of build 1.0 (6). Build 1.0 (8) addresses both issues.

GUIDELINE 3.1.1 — IN-APP PURCHASE
• Plus ($14.99/month) and Premium ($24.99/month) are now sold with Apple In-App Purchase as auto-renewable subscriptions in one group ("Old2New Membership"): app.old2new.ios.plus.monthly and app.old2new.ios.premium.monthly, submitted with this version.
• "Recipe Upgrades" is our name for a recipe transformation — the metered monthly unit (5/month free, 50 on Plus, 150 on Premium). It is not a separate product; the subscriptions are the only paid items. No credits, packs or one-time purchases.
• Paywall: sign in → Pricing tab. Also reached from the "Upgrade" button on Home, the "Plus or Premium plan required" card on Saved (PDF cookbook), and the modal shown when the 5 free transformations are used up.
• Purchase: tap "Subscribe to Plus" or "Subscribe to Premium"; the StoreKit sheet completes it, our server verifies the signed transaction and the plan updates immediately. Verified end to end in Sandbox with TestFlight build 8.
• Restore Purchases: Pricing screen (under the plan cards) and About → Subscription. Manage Subscription (Apple's sheet) is on the same screens for Apple-billed plans.
• Customers who subscribed on our website sign in with the same account and keep the plan they already pay for (3.1.3(b)). The app does not sell, link to or promote any other purchase method; a web-purchased plan shows "Purchased on old2new.app" as a plain label only.
• Subscription terms (title, length, price, auto-renewal, cancellation) and links to Terms of Use and Privacy Policy appear on the Pricing screen before purchase.

GUIDELINE 1.4.1 — HEALTH / NUTRITION INFORMATION
• Every health-related recommendation has a "Sources" control beside it that opens the supporting references (USDA FoodData Central, Dietary Guidelines for Americans, FDA, NIH/NIDDK, CDC, American Diabetes Association, peer-reviewed research); each is a working link.
• Sample path: Home → "What these mean" beside the goal chips explains each goal with sources. Enter "Chicken Alfredo", select "Diabetic Friendly", tap "Transform My Recipe". On the result: "How we estimate" under the before/after calories explains the methodology (AI-estimated, per serving, rounded, not measured) with sources; Swaps tab → "Sources" beside "Why These Swaps"; Macros tab → "How we estimate · Sources".
• Central references library: About → "Sources & References" → Open, or "View all references" from any Sources sheet. Also at https://old2new.app/references.
• Disclaimer on first launch (must be accepted), on every Sources sheet, in About, on the References page, the Pricing screen and the PDF cookbook: "Old2New provides general food and nutrition information for educational purposes only. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition. Nutrition values may be estimates and individual dietary needs vary. Consult a physician, registered dietitian, or other qualified healthcare professional before making dietary changes related to a medical condition or medication."
• All calorie and macro figures are labelled as estimates; AI text is constrained to ingredient characteristics and filtered server-side so it never claims to treat, prevent, control or be "safe for" any condition or medication.

TEST ACCOUNT
• The demo account above has a complimentary Premium membership so every feature (150 transformations/month, PDF cookbook) works without purchasing; Pricing shows the live subscriptions with Restore Purchases. To see the purchase sheet, create a new free account in the app (Get Started).

OTHER
• Recipes are generated by a third-party AI model (Anthropic); images by OpenAI. Account deletion: About → Delete Account. The PDF cookbook opens the system share sheet. Email confirmation and password-reset links return to the app via the old2new:// scheme.
